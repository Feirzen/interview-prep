TITLE: Day 12 · Interview morning warm-up
DATE: 2026-10-23
TYPE: Warm-up
SPEED 1.0

COACH: Good morning, Steven. It's interview day. This one's short. Just a warm-up, so you walk in loose and ready.
COACH: First, logistics, one last time. Fifty-seven Executive Park South, Suite three hundred. Not Clifton Road. Check in at eleven forty-five, and don't arrive more than ten minutes early. That means eleven thirty-five at the earliest. Free parking. Bring your luggage in if you need to. And put your phone on silent before you walk in.
COACH: Now, let's wake up your voice. Your proctor will ask the opener. Answer it out loud, the way you'll say it in the room.
PROCTOR: Good morning. So tell me about yourself.
PAUSE 90
COACH: Good. Present, moment, thread, destination. You've got it.
COACH: Here are three things to carry with you today. That's all. Three.
COACH: One. Be specific. Every time you're about to say something general, like "I love helping people," swap it for a moment. The ephedrine. The husband asking who the anesthesiologist was. The phone call. Specific beats impressive.
COACH: Two. Bridge it back. End your answers by connecting to the job. Safety, vigilance, the care team, the patient who can't speak for themselves.
COACH: Three. The whole three hours counts. The student Q and A, the tour, the refreshments. Be curious, be kind to the other candidates, and ask good questions. They're picking classmates, not just test-takers.
COACH: Now pick your anchor. One story you want them to hear today, no matter what they ask. For most people in your spot, it's the ephedrine moment, because it shows you understand the physiology and the role at the same time. Find a place for it.
COACH: Let's do one slow breathing round to settle your nerves. Breathe in for four counts. Hold for four. Out for four. Hold for four. I'll give you about twenty seconds.
PAUSE 20
COACH: Good. One more practice question, just to get the feel of a story.
PROCTOR: Tell me about a time you had to make a decision without all the information.
PAUSE 75
COACH: That's the housing scramble. Act on what's certain instead of freezing over what isn't. Today, that's your motto too. You can't control which questions they ask. You can control how you show up.
COACH: One last thing for after. Try to remember the names of the faculty in your breakout. Tonight or tomorrow, send a short thank-you email to the program. Mention one specific thing from the day.
COACH: You've put in the work, Steven. You've got real stories, real experience, and real reasons to be there. Go show them. Good luck today.
