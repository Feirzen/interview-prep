Don't ask (it's on the site)

  • How long is it? When does it start?
  • How big is the class? What's tuition?
  • What are the pass rates?
  • Which rotations are required?

Do ask

  • How are out-of-town rotations assigned, and how much say do students get?
  • What do students who thrive in year one do differently?
  • Does sim continue into the clinical year?
  • Students: where do people live, and how's the commute to sites?
