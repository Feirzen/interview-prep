How the 28 months are built

Early quarters · class first

Mostly classroom and sim
  • Runs on quarters, not semesters
  • A little OR time starts in quarter 1
  • Clinical hours ramp up quarter by quarter

Final year · all clinical

About 12 months
  • Month-long rotations, general plus subspecialties
  • Each one paired with a preceptor
  • 2,500+ clinical hours by graduation
Memory hook: 9 quarters, 28 months, 30 students, 2,500 hours.

Outcomes and history

  • 97.6% first-time pass rate · 94.4% graduation rate (classes of 2022 to 2024)
  • Program started in 2004. Savannah was South's first AA campus. Orlando and West Palm Beach came later
  • By 2018, South had seen an entire AA class pass the boards 11 times
  • Accredited by CAAHEP

Why it fits you

  • Georgia is a big CAA state with lots of jobs
  • Savannah is smaller and cheaper than Atlanta, and you and your wife both like warm weather
  • Fun link: Sherman's March to the Sea ended here, so it ties back to the Atlanta history you know

Don't ask (it's on the site)

Do ask
